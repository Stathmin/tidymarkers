# Z5.2 — Cas13a seed region: a position map and a manipulable 60-fold, not a bare penalty

Literature pass, 2026-11-03. Read from Europe PMC `fullTextXML` of
**PMC13136900** (all sections of the XML, including Methods and figure legends);
the paper has no Zotero item, so no lane was used for it. Rows:
`z5.2-cas13a-seed-kinetics.rows.csv`.

**Source.** Wei F _et al._ A kinetic approach for mapping seed regions of CRISPR
ribonucleoprotein and improving specificity. _Nucleic Acids Res_ 2026,
doi 10.1093/nar/gkag413, PMCID PMC13136900.

## 1. What it adds

`z5.2-crispr-seed-magnitude.md` found a per-position magnitude for Cas9 and
Cas12a (Jones 2021, `PMC9665413`) and a non-additive configuration-rate dataset
for Cas13a (Specht 2020, `PMC7260945`). This paper is the first **kinetic
mapping** of the Cas13a seed and the first reported seed for **LwaCas13a**:

| homolog | kinetically critical region (5′ spacer) | sets    |
| ------- | --------------------------------------- | ------- |
| LbuCas13a | 10–14 nt (overlap of 10–18, 8–16, 9–16, 7–14, 9–18) | 5 |
| LwaCas13a | 10–18 nt                               | 3       |

Lbu spacer is 20 nt, Lwa spacer 28 nt (23 nt for activator 3 in both).

## 2. What it does **not** give

- **No bare per-mismatch fold.** Without a "kinetic manipulator" the authors
  state a single mismatch produces a "minor change in binding kinetics and
  energy … insufficient to differentiate the mismatch and PM RNA targets"
  (Fig. 5A/C/E, Suppl. Fig. S18). The only bare number printed is a best-case
  discrimination factor of **1.49** (Fig. 6D, Lbu, 20 mismatch positions).
- The headline numbers are **manipulator** gains, not mismatch penalties:
  DF 1.49 → **33** (22-fold), binding slowed "over 60-fold" (Suppl. Fig. S17).
  A manipulator is a designed DNA blocker with Tm tuned above 30 °C for the MM
  target and below 20 °C for the PM target; those Tm are **IDT OligoAnalyzer
  estimates**, not measurements.
- Cas13a is PAM/PFS-free, so none of this constrains `family_crispr_pam`.

## 3. Verdict for `crispr_seed_rule`

**Still `unknown` for the magnitude, now with a mapped axis.** The literature
supports *where* the Cas13a seed is (10–14 / 10–18) and that a bare single
mismatch inside it is **not** a usable fold signal without an engineered
blocker. It does not support a per-position weight. The prior Cas9/Cas12a rows
(`PMC9665413`: >100-fold at positions 1–9, ~10⁻¹–10⁻³ s⁻¹ at 10–17, no effect
at 18–20) remain the only numeric seed magnitudes, and they apply to a
different effector family.

Two rows are usable as *bounds* rather than weights: bare Cas13a single-mismatch
DF **1.49** (an upper bound on what a bare seed mismatch buys in a Cas13a
fluorescence assay) and the **10–14 nt** overlap as the seed **window** for any
future positional model.

## 4. Caveats

- One lab, one paper, Lbu homolog for the DF numbers; Lwa seed is 3 activator
  sets only, and the authors themselves call the Lwa region "kinetically
  sensitive" rather than the seed.
- The kinetic quantity (binding rate constant, reporter-cleavage initial
  velocity) is **not** the quantity `specificity_policy()` models (a
  mismatch-count penalty on detection success). Converting between them is not
  licensed by this paper.
- Aggregated in-repo bundles built by `data-raw/proposed/*.rows.csv` do not
  change; this file is additive and unwired.
