# Z8.2 / Z8.4 — soybean per-assay outcome: what exists, what is missing

Status: searched 2026-10-29 (agent A1). **No failure-complete soybean per-assay table
found.** Two public soybean sources give a denominator small enough to read per assay,
and both only ever report working assays. The operator's own panel (36 assays) is still
the largest soybean per-assay table, and since 2026-11-03 it carries an operator-supplied
`outcome` column: all 36 `worked`, 0 `failed`.

## 1. What the soybean worked panel DOES supply

`data-raw/soybean_worked_panel.R` → `inst/extdata/soybean_worked_panel_*.csv`, from
"Напоминалка по праймерам" (36 assays, 85 oligos, build GCF_000004515.6):

| scheme            | assays |
| ----------------- | ------ |
| `sanger_amplicon` | 15     |
| `sanger_dt2_body` | 8      |
| `kasp`            | 10     |
| `kasp_dt2_flank`  | 3      |
| **total**         | **36** |

i.e. 23 Sanger amplicons and 13 KASP sets. Every assay carries sequences, Tm, product
size / KASP gap, printed GBS class counts and exact-genome-hit counts. The 13 KASP sets
are graded against the package's own acceptance gates
(`soybean_worked_panel_kasp_gates.csv`). That is a **design benchmark**: it proves the
package reproduces the arithmetic and the Tm of a panel that was actually ordered.

## 2. What it does NOT supply

`inst/extdata/soybean_worked_panel_assays.csv` has `status`:

- 32 rows — `NA`
- 4 rows — `"redesigned after external check"`
- **36 rows — a wet-lab outcome** (`worked`), added 2026-11-03 by the operator

The panel's own header says nothing about bench results. "Redesigned after external
check" is a _design revision_, not "the assay failed at the bench". The operator has
since stated that every assay was ordered, attempted and produced a distinct signal;
`basis` quotes that statement and is not a transcription of bench data.

byte-identical to `HEAD`. Values actually written:

| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- | ----------------------------- |
| `worked`                                                        | 36    | operator statement 2026-11-03 |
| `failed` / `ambiguous` / `no_amplification` / `no_polymorphism` | **0** | —                             |

One basis text, with an appended clause where the panel's own status matters:

- 24 rows — "operator statement 2026-11-03: assay was ordered, attempted and worked on
  the soybean panel, producing a distinct signal".
- 4 rows (`status = "redesigned after external check"`) — same, plus "(revised design;
  operator reports the revised assay worked)".

  operator reports it was ordered anyway)".

The 13 KASP rows say "a distinct KASP signal"; the 23 Sanger rows say "a distinct
amplicon signal".

Harness (Z8.2): `dev/targets/_targets.R` adds `panel_outcomes()`, which joins these
labels to `soybean_worked_panel_kasp_gates.csv` and writes
`inst/extdata/z8_gate_outcomes.csv` — a table, never a rate. Current content: 36 rows,
`outcome = worked` × 36, `gate_status = fail` × 13 (the 13 KASP sets),
`not_evaluated` × 23 (the Sanger assays, which no gate grades). Guarded: closed
vocabulary, `outcome`/`basis` NA together, no `failed` without a `basis`, unique
`(source, row)`. A re-run reproduces the file byte-for-byte. The artefact is excluded
from the harness's own input list so the harness never depends on its last result, and
the two input CSVs are `format = "file"` targets so editing one invalidates the join.

Split (Z8.4): `tests/testthat/test-z8-outcomes.R` keys every outcome row by
`(source, row)` (`operator_panel_soybean` / `literature:<study>`) and asserts that a
source-level split has an empty intersection; the same test shows a row-level split
leaks a source across both sides.

### Columns and rules

| new column | allowed values                                                                               | meaning                                                                           |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `outcome`  | `worked` / `failed` / `ambiguous` / `no_amplification` / `no_polymorphism` / `not_attempted` | what the bench said for that assay                                                |
| `basis`    | free text                                                                                    | the observation it rests on (band score, cluster plot, Ct, call rate, plate/date) |

Rules that keep it honest:

- `assay_id` is the join key and is unique in the panel — no new identifier needed.
- Anything not ordered or not run stays `not_attempted`, never `failed`.
- A design revision (`redesigned after external check`) is its own code, not a failure.
- `basis` is per row: a rate without the observation behind it is not citable under `ZW4`.

With that file, Z8.4's split is `source ∈ {operator panel, public literature}`, never
row-level; and Z8.2's harness has a per-assay label to fit against the gate verdicts.
That is now in place. What remains is **discrimination**: every row is `worked`, so the
harness has outcomes but no negative class, and no gate-pass → outcome rate may be
computed until `Z8.1` supplies failures. `conversion_rates.csv` still has 3 decomposed
rows from 3 studies.

## 4. Public soybean per-assay data that does exist (small)

| source                                           | per-assay reading                                                                                                  | limit                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Meng 2026, `10.3390/plants15162479`, PMC13516154 | 6 SNPs prioritised, **2 submitted to KASP, both worked**; screening efficiency 19/26 = 73.08 % and 26/35 = 74.29 % | no failure; the other 4 were never submitted → `not_attempted`                              |
| Rosso 2021, `10.3390/ijms22052675`, PMC7961957   | **3 of 5** SNPs converted to KASP, all polymorphic on 93 PIs; selection efficiency 64 % / 86 % / 31 %              | the 2 non-converted SNPs were unlinked/monomorphic in the mapping parents, not PCR failures |
| Meng 2026 screens                                | 308 of 336 accessions gave unambiguous homozygous calls at **both** loci                                           | a call-rate, not an assay outcome                                                           |

Machine-readable extract: `data-raw/meng2026_soybean_outcomes.R` →
`inst/extdata/meng2026_soybean_per_assay.csv`, 6 rows (2 `worked`, 4 `not_attempted`).
It is a conversion _denominator_ of 6 with 2 successes and **zero failures**, so it can
anchor "KASP conversion from a prioritised SNP list is not automatic" but it cannot
calibrate a failure rate. Rosso's 3/5 is not written as rows: the two non-converted SNPs
were dropped for linkage/polymorphism in the parents, and the paper names neither their
ids nor a failed assay.

## 5. Consequences for the roadmap (for the owner to apply — not edited here)

- `Z8.2` has the harness and the operator label; all 36 rows are `worked`, so it remains
  _uncalibrated_ until a failure-complete source supplies the negative class. It is not
  blocked on more literature: the soybean KASP literature reports developed markers,
  not failed ones.
- `Z8.4` stays blocked on `Z8.2`, as stated.
- `inst/extdata/conversion_rates.csv` is **not** edited by this agent. If Meng 2026 and
  Rosso 2021 are wanted there, the owner of `data-raw/mismatch-discrimination.R` must add
  them; note that neither has a failure count, so both would enter as `n_failed = 0` rows
  and would pull any pooled rate up — that is exactly the Z8.3 leakage trap.
