# Z8.2 — soybean per-assay outcomes: new search (2026-10-29)

Companion to `z8.2-soybean-outcome-gap.md` (unchanged, evidence). Scope: literature
only; the operator table is not touched.

Question: can a **gate pass** be paired with a **per-assay bench verdict** (worked /
failed, with a cause) for soybean, or failing that, for any crop?

Reading rule used here: a usable pair needs, per locus/assay, (a) a prediction stage
(designed, passed in-silico design, passed a gate) and (b) a bench verdict for **that
same assay**. A paper that reports only assays that worked supplies no negative class.

## Soybean — what was searched and what came back

Europe PMC query (run twice, 2026-10-29):

```
(soybean OR "Glycine max")
AND (KASP OR "kompetitive allele")
AND ("did not amplify" OR "no amplification" OR "no signal" OR monomorphic
     OR "poor clustering" OR clustered OR "off-target")
```

484 hits. The soybean subset was read in full text for a failure class. Result:

**Zero soybean papers print a per-assay failure breakdown.** Every soybean KASP paper
in the hit set reports only markers that were developed and validated:

| source | what it prints | why it cannot calibrate a failure rate |
|---|---|---|
| PMC12689555 (2025) pod traits | "a KASP marker, S20_35808042 (G/C), was developed and successfully validated in 97 soybean accessions" | one marker, no failures → no negative class |
| PMC11466877 (2024) shade tolerance | "four KASP markers … have been successfully applied (Table 7; Figure 7)" | four markers, no failures |
| PMC10879362 (2024) salt tolerance | "KASP … markers were successfully designed to genotype soybean accessions" | no denominator, no failures |
| PMC11640847 (2024) FORR | one KASP + two CAPS, clustering "consistent with disease resistance" | no denominator, no failures |
| PMC12197188 (2025) GmPDH1/SCN | one KASP M0526; "identification efficiencies for resistant and susceptible lines were 90.47% and 71.25%" | these are genotype–phenotype concordances for a working marker, not assay outcomes |
| PMC11836386 (2025) qSCN10 | "We developed additional KASP markers (Table S3) from the fine-mapped region" | no failure class |
| PMC13616858 (2026) Rdm3 | three KASP assays (GSM1395, GSM933, GSM1402), all used across five populations | no failure class; per-population "missing" is segregation/monomorphism, not an assay verdict |
| PMC11728539 (2024) seed protein | "Nineteen markers with high genotyping efficacy were identified through screening"; 102 candidate genes → 67 SNPs (Table S2) | denominator is *loci*, not assays; 19 is the surviving set; no failure cause printed |

One soybean source has a **partial** decomposition and is the closest thing to a
negative class found in soybean: **PMC13264645** (RenSeq-derived SNPs → KASP for
*Phytophthora sojae* resistance, 2026, `10.1007/s11032-026-01686-1`). Table 2 lists 12
RenSeq-derived SNPs across five RIL populations with "mapped" / "-" cells, and the
footnote states:

> "*Williams82 genome assembly v4.0 – indicates that a SNP was not mapped in that
> population due to being **monomorphic and/or not having a gene of interest** on that
> chromosome"

and the Methods:

> "The resultant KASP primers were verified on DNA samples from the two parental lines
> of each RIL population and **those showing clear unambiguous separation between
> calling genotypes were used for mapping** the RenSeq variants"

This is a gate (parental unambiguous separation) followed by a verdict, but (a) the
verdict is recorded **per population**, not per assay, (b) the failing class is
compound ("monomorphic and/or not having a gene of interest"), and (c) the number of
assays designed is not printed (the 12 in Table 2 are the ones kept). It cannot be
counted; it is the only soybean source that even has the shape.

## Conclusion for the operator's 36-assay panel

The soybean literature cannot supply the Z8.2 negative class. What it supplies, at
best, is a *positive-only* set: markers that were designed, genotyped and reported.
A panel scored against that literature would have 36 positives and 0 negatives — which
by construction cannot estimate a failure rate, only a "markers that get published
work" selection effect.

The genuinely usable calibration material is non-soybean and study-level:
**268 → 258 (no signal, 10)** for *Brassica rapa*, **100 → 71 (21 compound
monomorphic/heterozygous, 8 no amplification)** for maize, and **4,000 → 3,366** for
rice with an unattributed failure class but a published predictor-vs-outcome table
(Table 2) that is the only found instance of the exact Z8.2 pairing at scale. None of
these is a soybean per-assay panel.

## Still missing

1. **The `outcome` column in the operator's 36-assay soybean panel** — unchanged by
   this search. Without a bench verdict per assay (worked / failed + cause) the gate
   cannot be scored on it. The earlier gap document's proposed `outcome`/`basis`
   columns remain the required change.
2. **Any soybean paper with a printed assay denominator and a per-assay failure
   verdict.** Not found. The four candidate shapes were: a failure breakdown
   (none), a per-assay table (none in an article body; soybean Supplementary Tables
   were not reachable for PMC13264645 in XML — only the article body table),
   a compound failure footnote (PMC13264645, per population), and a
   genotype–phenotype concordance (PMC12197188, not an assay outcome).
3. **A "poor clustering" negative class for any crop.** The maize row is a compound
   monomorphic/heterozygous class; the cabbage row is no-signal only; the rice row is
   unattributed. No source found prints a count of assays failed specifically for poor
   or ambiguous clustering.
4. **A per-assay table, in any crop, that names the assay and its verdict.** No such
   article-body table was found in this sweep. If one is needed, the next step is to
   check supplementary files of Steele et al. 2025 (Supplementary File S2, "Tables I
   and J") and Shimizu et al. 2025 (Supplemental Table 2) directly — both are outside
   the XML and were not read here.
